---
description: Design and optimize user experiences specifically for people with Chronic Fatigue Syndrome (CFS), minimizing cognitive load and interaction effort. Use when designing flows, interactions, or UX decisions.
---

This skill ensures all product decisions are aligned with the realities of CFS:

- fatigue
- brain fog
- limited energy
- inconsistent daily capacity

---

## Core Principle

Design for the user's **worst day**, not their best.

---

## UX Thinking

Before designing:

- What happens if the user is exhausted?
- Can this be completed in under 5 seconds?
- Can this be skipped without penalty?

---

## Interaction Rules

- 1-tap actions preferred
- Voice input supported where possible
- Backfill allowed (no rigid timelines)
- No forced daily streaks or pressure

---

## Cognitive Load Reduction

- Limit visible options
- Use progressive disclosure
- Avoid complex data visualization

---

## Emotional Design

- Neutral, supportive tone
- No guilt or pressure
- No "failure states"

---

## Anti-Patterns

- Over-tracking
- Data overload
- Frequent notifications
- Rigid workflows

---

## Output Format

- Provide UX decisions or flows
- Explain reasoning in terms of CFS constraints

---

## Goal

Create an experience that remains usable even during crashes or severe fatigue.
