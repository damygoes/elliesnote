This skill ensures data consistency, safety, and scalability.

---

## Modeling Thinking

Before defining types:

- What data is essential?
- What can be optional?
- What needs validation?

---

## Rules

- Use TypeScript types + Zod schemas together
- Avoid loose structures
- Ensure forward compatibility

---

## Validation

- All external or stored data must be validated
- Use Zod for runtime validation

---

## Design Principles

- Keep models minimal
- Avoid deeply nested structures
- Prefer flat and predictable shapes

---

## Output Format

- TypeScript type
- Zod schema
- Brief explanation

---

## Goal

Ensure reliable, predictable, and safe data handling across the app.
